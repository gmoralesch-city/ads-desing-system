The "Servicios" brand icon treatment: a line-icon subject inside a circular cyan or navy chip, optional label below. Used across product grids and the app home.

```jsx
<ServiceIcon tone="cyan" icon={<Car/>} label="Vehicular" />
```

The Figma "Servicios" set is proprietary duotone art — export those PNGs from Figma for pixel-accuracy; this component approximates them with Lucide line icons in a cyan chip.
